Plant Motion Analysis

In demo mode, you may only play an already traced movie. You may also move markers, but the movement won't be saved. When logged in as a user (not in demo mode), you may add and delete markers, position them and have their new positions saved, and trace and re-trace movies. When in demo mode, these features are disabled.

To analyze the motion of the plant in the video:

  • Place the Apex marker on the plant's apex
  • Move the Ruler markers to the corresponding parts of the ruler in the video (if there is a ruler in the video). The initial marker names Ruler 0mm and Ruler 10mm mean that the two ruler points are 10mm apart. The difference between the marker values should match the distance on the ruler. Use Ruler 0mm and Ruler 30mm or Ruler 20mm and Ruler 50mm for points 30 mm apart. Use the pencil icon in the Marker Table to rename a ruler marker.
  • Press the trace or retrace button to follow the markers through the last included frame.

You may add additional markers to trace additional points of interest.

Once upload processing finishes, you may browse the entire movie using the controls on the movie player section of the page. You may play the whole movie from the current point, step forwards or backwards one frame at a time, or go to a specified frame.

If a marker drifts, go to the frame where it lost the feature and reposition it. Use the retrace button to resume from that frame through the last included frame.

Units and Conventions

Pixel coordinates are relative to the origin point (0,0) in the lower-left corner of the frame. Coordinate values increase from left to right and bottom to top.

In the Marker Table Location (mm) column, millimeter values are expressed using the same lower-left coordinate system after ruler calibration.

The position graphs show each marker's horizontal and vertical change from its first saved position in the selected frame range.
